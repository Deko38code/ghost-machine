Why this matters

Costs you absorb stay invisible. Every dollar of hosting, email and AI you pay for a client is a dollar of margin you never see on an invoice.

Support time is the real cost. A care plan that clears $200 but eats six hours pays about $33 an hour. Decide whether that is the rate you want before you sell it again.

Price the plan against the work, not the market. What the client pays for is uptime, fixes and someone answering — not the server bill.
