  1. Put the coach one tap from every admin screen. Six turns a month is a discovery problem before it is a value problem — the coach is reachable, but not from the pages where a decision gets made (Bookings, Spam, Analytics).
  2. Open it pre-loaded with the screen's own numbers. A coach that starts with "your busiest day is Saturday, 3 unread spam rows, $0 collected this month" gets used; a blank box does not.
  3. Ship three saved prompts the client would actually ask: what should I price this job at, who has not rebooked, what changed this week.
  4. One weekly nudge carrying the three numbers that moved, with a link straight into the coach — measured from real rows, never a fabricated percentage (§10).
  5. Report the value back, not the cost. Show saved-value against the $249 plan on the client side; keep our cost and margin owner-only (already enforced — Junkalot's /admin/value is a 404 and the record lives in HaksterAI's owner admin).

Advice only. None of this changes a model, a route or a price (§19); each item would be built and verified as its own change, on Junkalot's own build, from its own master copy.
