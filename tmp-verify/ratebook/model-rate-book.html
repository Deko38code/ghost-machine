What this means for the cards

Recorded for the price sheet. Nothing here changes a model, a route or a price — the setup stays exactly as it is.

Our Lite card sells 30 AI calls + 10 web lookups a month. At 2,500 tokens a turn, those 30 calls cost us $0 in plan credits. The same 30 calls on Sonnet 5.5 would be $0.27, on Opus 5.5 $0.54, on Fable 5.2 $1.08 — a third of a $3.45 Lite month before hosting.

That is the whole argument for the cheap lane: the card price is not carried by the model bill. It also means we never need to touch the model setup to protect a margin.

Watch list

  • Gemini 3 Flash, $0.50 / $3.00. The closest thing to a premium brand at Haiku money. Worth a look only if our lane ever runs out of credits — not a swap, a spare.
  • DeepSeek direct API, half price off-peak. 01:00–04:00 and 06:00–10:00 UTC are off-peak. Our cloud account already bills nothing per token, so this matters only as a fallback.
  • Haiku 5.5 at $0.10 / $0.50. Identical list price to gpt-6-luna. If we ever needed a real Claude-brand lane for a client, this is the one that does not move the card.
  • Groq gpt-oss-20b at $0.075 / $0.30. The absolute cheapest metered lane found today. Speed is its selling point, quality is not — kept as a last-resort fallback in HaksterAI's waterfall.
